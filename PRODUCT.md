# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, Motion, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis. Deployed on Vercel. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

- Attendees: people in Bhubaneswar (students, young professionals, families) deciding what to do tonight or this weekend, usually on a phone.
- Organizers: small hosts (venues, collectives, college clubs, artists) who need to publish an event, sell tiers of tickets and see who is coming.

## Product Purpose

A city events platform for Bhubaneswar: discover events (music, dance, food, talks, workshops, sport, festivals), book tickets, and host and manage your own events. Success: a visitor finds something for a date and books it in under a minute; an organizer publishes an event in one sitting.

## Positioning

Built for one city, not a national listings feed. Events are framed by Bhubaneswar's own calendar, venues and neighbourhoods.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Browse, search and filter events by category, date, area and price.
- Event detail with schedule, venue, ticket tiers and booking; booking issues a ticket with a QR code.
- Organizer flow: create, edit and delete events; see tickets sold.
- No backend: bookings and organizer-created events persist in the browser (localStorage). Confirmed by the user.
- No payments: booking is a free demo checkout and must be labelled as such.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real customers, partners, press, reviews or business statistics. None may be invented.
- Sample events are demonstration content and must be labelled as such on the site and in the README.
- No product photography; imagery comes from openly licensed collections (credited) or is drawn in code.

## Product Principles

1. The city is the identity: its places, calendar and crafts, not generic nightlife stock.
2. Finding and booking must be quicker than reading about it.
3. Hosting is a first-class path, not an afterthought behind a footer link.
4. Honest demo: anything sample or simulated says so.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-complete booking and hosting flows, visible focus, reduced-motion support, phone-first layouts.
